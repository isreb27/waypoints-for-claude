# Privacy Policy: Waypoints for Claude

**Effective date:** September 25, 2026

Waypoints is a browser extension that helps you find your way around your own conversations on claude.ai. This policy explains exactly what it stores and what it doesn't.

## In short

- Everything Waypoints stores stays in your browser, on your device.
- Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else. Waypoints has no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- It runs only on claude.ai, and never in private or incognito windows.

## What Waypoints stores

Only information about the messages **you send** on claude.ai while Waypoints is installed:

- the chat's ID and title
- for each message you send: the first 300 characters, a short fingerprint of the text, and the time you sent it
- for messages you save: up to 600 characters, plus any note you add
- your settings, such as display options and keyboard shortcuts

This is kept in the browser's local extension storage. It isn't synced to other devices, and Claude's replies are never stored.

## What Waypoints does not do

- It doesn't send, sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
- It doesn't access other websites, your browsing history, or your Claude account settings.
- It doesn't use your data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or anything unrelated to its features.
- It doesn't load or run code from the internet.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| --- | --- |
| Access to claude.ai | To show the message rail and panel on claude.ai, and to read the messages you send there. No other site. |
| Storage | To keep send times, saved messages and settings on your device. |
| Unlimited storage | So long-time users can keep more than the browser's default 10 MB. It doesn't change where the data is kept. |

## Your control

- **See it:** the Waypoints panel shows everything stored (History and Saved tabs).
- **Export it:** Settings → Export backup saves it all to a file you keep.
- **Delete it:** Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the extension also removes everything it stored.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received through Waypoints adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date before the change ships.
